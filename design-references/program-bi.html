Business Incubation Program

Jagoan Tani Banyuwangi

Jagoan Tani Banyuwangi is a business incubation program that empowers young agricultural entrepreneurs to develop innovative and sustainable agribusiness ideas. Through mentorship, hands-on learning, and business development, participants are guided to transform agricultural challenges into viable business opportunities and scalable solutions.

Business Incubation Program

Kita Tani Muda Semarang

Kita Tani Muda Semarang is a business incubation program designed to empower young entrepreneurs in developing innovative and sustainable agricultural businesses. Through mentorship, practical learning, and business development, participants are guided to turn their ideas into viable solutions and create meaningful impact in the agricultural sector.
